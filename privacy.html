Sprigette Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026 Applies to: Sprigette for Android (com.sprigette.plantid), version 1.x Published by: Book Weaver Publishing, LLC

Sprigette identifies plants on your device and stores your garden locally. Optional crash reports and problem reports help us fix failures.

Optional crash reports

Crash reporting is off until you turn on Settings → Help and reporting → Send crash reports. When enabled and configured, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives technical failure details, stack traces, app version, device and operating-system information, an installation identifier, and app session timing used to measure crash frequency. We use this information to investigate failures. We do not attach your photos, garden records, purchase tokens, or contact details. Exception messages may contain information about the operation that failed. Google processes this information under its Firebase privacy and security information.

You can turn reporting off in Settings. Future automatic uploads stop after the app restarts. A report already uploading may finish. Turning reporting off does not recall reports already sent. Your reporting choice is not included in garden backups or phone-to-phone transfers. Reporting starts off on a new installation.

The app has network permission for crash reporting. Plant identification stays on your device. We do not include Google Analytics, advertising, or a garden cloud-sync service.

Reports you send yourself

Settings → Help and reporting → Report a problem opens a form. You can describe the problem and optionally include the app and Android versions. It opens an email draft to support@sprigette.com. Review it and send it in your email app. Opening a draft does not send it. No photos, garden records, purchase details, or logs are attached automatically. Your email provider handles delivery. We receive the message and sender details when you send it, and use them to investigate and reply. Do not include passwords or payment details. You can copy the report if no email app is available.

Photos

  • Plant identification runs on your device using a bundled machine-learning model. Identification does not upload your photos. Saved photos may leave your device through Android backups or a backup file you export, as explained below.
  • Photos you capture or import are stored in Sprigette's private app storage. Other apps cannot read them, and they are not added to your gallery.
  • Before a photo is saved, Sprigette re-encodes it and strips all metadata, including GPS location and capture-time EXIF tags. A photo imported from your gallery loses its location data the moment Sprigette stores its own copy. (This behavior is covered by automated tests in our build.)
  • You can turn off photo retention entirely with Settings → Identification → Save photos to history.

Data stored on your device

Your garden, care schedules, scan history, photos, and preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage.

Backups

Sprigette gives you two ways to keep a copy of your garden. Both are yours. Neither backup option sends your garden to us.

A backup file you make yourself. Settings → Backup → Back up my garden writes your plants, care history, and photos into a single file, and Android's own save dialog asks you where to put it. If you choose a folder belonging to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or any other app on your phone, that app uploads the file — Sprigette does not upload the backup itself. Once the file leaves the app, the privacy policy that governs it is the one belonging to whatever service you chose.

Android's own backup. Sprigette allows Android Auto Backup, the system feature that copies an app's data to your Google account so it returns when you set up a new phone. This is operated by Google, not by us: the data is stored in your Google Drive backup space, and we have no access to it and no way to read it. Sprigette only lets Android make this backup when Android can encrypt it end to end with your phone's screen lock (your PIN, pattern, or password). If your phone has no screen lock, or runs Android 8, Android does not back Sprigette up to your Google account at all. You can turn it off for every app or for Sprigette alone in Android Settings → Google → Backup, and Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Auto Backup has a size limit of 25 MB per app; if your photos take you past it, Android skips the backup, which is one reason the backup file above is the more reliable of the two.

Moving to a new phone. On Android 9 or later, when you move to a new phone with a cable or a direct phone-to-phone transfer, Android can copy the same data straight to the new phone. That copy goes from one phone to the other, not through Google's servers, so it does not need a screen lock.

Restoring a backup adds to your garden — it never replaces it. Nothing already in your garden is deleted by a restore.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph plants. Optional: you can use gallery photos instead.
  • Notifications — to deliver care reminders. Optional and skippable.
  • Run at boot — to re-schedule the daily care-reminder digest after a restart.
  • Network access — for optional crash reports.

Sprigette requests no location, storage, or contacts permissions.

Purchases

Sprigette offers an optional subscription ("Sprigette Premium"), billed monthly or annually and processed entirely by Google Play. Sprigette also supports restoring a one-time Premium unlock if your Google Play account holds one. Sprigette does not receive your card or bank account details. Google's handling of billing data is described in the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. The app stores signed purchase evidence locally, plus purchase tokens needed to acknowledge purchases through the Play Store app. It refreshes purchase status with Google Play when it can connect. If Google Play cannot be reached, previously verified evidence may keep Premium enabled until a successful refresh. Restoring a backup does not establish that the current Google account owns an active subscription.

If you use the optional in-app rating prompt or the "Rate Sprigette" link, that interaction is handled by the Google Play Store app under Google's policies.

Deleting your data

You can erase your garden, care schedules, scan history, photos, and preferences with Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app. The in-app deletion also turns crash reporting off.

Local deletion does not erase problem emails or crash reports already sent. Contact support@sprigette.com about those reports. Reports may not contain enough information to link them to a particular person. Backup files and Android Auto Backup copies are separate. Remove them through the service where you saved them or Android's backup settings.

Children

Sprigette is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our data handling changes. The app settings and store declarations must match the version we release.
