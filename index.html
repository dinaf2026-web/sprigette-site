Sprigette

A plant identifier and plant-care app for Android that runs entirely on your device. It ships with 3,093 plant records and identifies plants without a network connection.

Sprigette collects no data. It requests no internet permission at all, so the operating system itself prevents the app from sending anything off your phone. There is no account, no cloud sync, no analytics and no advertising.
